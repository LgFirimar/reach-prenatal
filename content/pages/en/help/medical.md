**The REACH clinic is not for emergencies.** It is open only on clinic days.
**Your safety comes first, even if you are worried about cost.**

:::box{tone="red"}
## 🚨 Call 911 now

- Heavy bleeding
- Fainting or collapse
- Seizure (fit)
- Very hard to breathe
- Chest pain
- The baby is coming now

Call :data[emergency.911]
:::

:::box{tone="orange"}
## 🏥 Go to the hospital now

- Bleeding from the vagina
- Fluid leaking from the vagina (your water breaks)
- Regular, painful contractions, or signs of labour. This is extra important before 37 weeks.
- The baby moves less, or not at all
- A bad headache that does not go away
- Changes in how you see (blurry, spots, flashing lights)
- Pain under the right ribs or in the upper belly
- Bad pain in the belly
- Fever
- Vomiting a lot, and you cannot keep fluids down

**If you have a midwife practice,** call your midwife's 24/7 number first for labour and urgent problems.
:::

## 💬 Mental health crisis

- If you might hurt yourself, call or text :data[emergency.crisis_988] (Suicide Crisis Helpline).
- If you are in danger now, call :data[emergency.911].
