You can give birth in a **hospital**, a **birth centre**, or at **home**.
You and your midwife decide together. It depends on what you want and what is safe for you.

## Hospital

- Most births in Toronto are in a hospital. :todo[Check]
- **The hospital bed and room are not covered.** Some midwife practices have agreements that lower this fee. :todo[Amounts, e.g. about CAD 1,000 per night]
