## What happens

- The midwife gets to know you and asks about your health and past pregnancies.
- She checks your blood pressure.
- Depending on how many weeks you are, she listens to the baby's heartbeat or measures your belly.
- Visits can take longer when a phone interpreter is used.

## Papers you get

- **Forms for blood tests, urine tests and ultrasound** (called "requisitions").
- **A billing letter.** It tells the lab or ultrasound clinic to bill REACH, not you. :todo[Exact name of the billing letter]

## Where to do the tests

REACH has no lab or ultrasound on site. You can go to any LifeLabs, Dynacare or ultrasound clinic. Bring the billing letter every time. :todo[Do all locations accept the letter? Which ones know it well?]

## Cost

**You pay nothing** for tests ordered by REACH.
