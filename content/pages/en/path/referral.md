## Why we refer you

- Ontario's midwifery program pays for pregnancy care for people without OHIP. :todo[Confirm wording and scope with AOM]
- This includes midwife visits, lab tests, ultrasounds, the birth, and a doctor (obstetrician or anesthesia) if you need one.
- Many midwife practices have agreements with their hospital that lower hospital fees. :todo[Which practices; amounts]
- You get this funding by going to a midwife practice first, even if you later also need an obstetrician.

## What happens

- REACH sends the referral. You do not need to apply yourself.
- The midwife practice will call or email you. The number may be one you don't know. :todo[Confirm how practices contact patients]
- **If another clinic calls you, take the appointment.**
- Once a practice takes you, you don't need to come back to REACH.

## What if no practice has a spot yet?

It is harder to find a spot late in pregnancy, and for some due-date months. :todo[Which months]
**You keep coming to REACH until a practice takes you.**
