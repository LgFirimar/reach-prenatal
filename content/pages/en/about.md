This app helps people who come to the REACH prenatal clinic at Access Alliance in Toronto.
It explains the steps of your care, what is free, and what to do in an emergency.

## Your privacy

- You don't need an account.
- Anything you type stays **only on this phone**. The app does not send it to anyone.
- You can delete it any time on the page "My data on this phone".

## Important

- This app gives general information. It does not replace your midwife or doctor.
- **It is not for emergencies. Call 911.**
- Information can change. If something is not clear, ask the clinic staff.
